## Reconstruction

`studies/reception.ts` recreates frames396–985, 19.667 seconds, at1920×1080. It uses editable DOM typography and chat/cards, SVG rings/icons, analytic gradient materials, deterministic dot texture, geometric transforms and one frame-driven Hyperframes clock. Reference pixels are excluded from the generated picture. The separate comparison artifact labels the original on the left and the reconstruction on the right.

The reconstruction deliberately tests the difficult transitions: warm circle → luminous sphere → avatar and full-frame material → glass conversation → contracting outline → lit cards → flattened band → wordmark. The material flow is an approximation, not the original physical shading or shader; the telephone/collage opening is documented but outside the replicated excerpt. A successful export does not establish production parity or automatic URL-to-film generation.
