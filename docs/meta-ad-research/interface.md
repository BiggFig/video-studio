# Six software video-ad references

Observed 2026-10-03. Six distinct creatives across Slack, Figma, Grammarly and Canva. One direct Meta record and five Motion public-archive records. Two aspect-ratio versions of the same Figma creative were deliberately counted once. Full videos remain in ignored local research; public assets are six low-resolution criticism excerpts per creative.

## Method and limits

Inspected decoded frames every 0.5 seconds over every complete file, plus every 0.25 seconds through the opening three seconds. Scene ranges below are approximate editorial intervals, not native-frame cut measurements. No normal-speed viewing or listening claim. No spend, conversion or comparative-performance claim. Archive summaries helped locate candidates but were not used as substitutes for watching the frames.

## First changes worth making

1. Start with relevant friction or a result object, with movement from frame zero.
2. Make the attention-grab hand off to the same real task/UI object.
3. Use one decisive macro camera move on the actual action; settle on the changed result.
4. Preserve a readable result and short CTA; copying a reference’s long held end card or tool-menu exploration would work against this brief.
