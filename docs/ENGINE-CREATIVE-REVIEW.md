# Engine creative review

Use this rubric on the **actual exported film**, after technical checks pass. Export integrity, valid source bindings and a successful render do not establish creative quality. Browser-to-export SSIM measures rendering consistency; it is not a reference-similarity or preference score.

Record the output path/hash, plan version, reference and compared range, reviewer, date, frames/timecodes inspected, and whether the entire film was watched and heard. Mark each area **pass**, **rework**, or **not reviewed**, with one concrete observation. Do not invent a numerical grade. A sampled still review cannot pass pacing or sound.

## Review questions

| Area | Pass question | Rework evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Audience and product comprehension** | After one uninterrupted watch, can someone unfamiliar with the product name its intended user, the task it helps with, and the benefit—using what the film actually shows? Is the main claim supported by captured evidence? | Generic “work smarter” copy; an audience label contradicted by the demonstration; a product name without a comprehensible mechanism; an unsupported promise. Ask the viewer to state their understanding before explaining the film. |
| **Action → result** | Can the viewer follow a specific input or choice, the action taken, and a visibly different resulting state? Is the result held long enough to understand? | A cursor moves without causing anything; an outcome resets to the initial state; a benefit headline substitutes for the actual result; unrelated screens imply a workflow that was never supported. |
| **UI fidelity** | Against the captured source, are essential labels, controls, grouping, proportions, colors and selected states faithful? Are observed states distinguished in the evidence from supported illustrative examples? | Invented controls or success states, rewritten product labels, generic accent overriding the source selection style, duplicate overlaid text, illegible targets, or an action on a hidden control. Inspect the input, action midpoint/end and final state at full size. Decorative staging must remain visibly outside the product interface. |
| **Hierarchy and type** | At delivery size, does the eye reach the intended message and active control first? Are line breaks, scale, contrast and reading holds deliberate? Does staged type resolve into stable, complete words? | Dense source text overwhelms the mechanism; competing headline, logo and UI; small text enlarged beyond its useful resolution; clipping, awkward line breaks or movement continuing through the essential reading hold. |
| **Materials and depth** | Do lighting, edges, shadows, grain and layering give objects a coherent physical character while supporting the product? Is the foreground/background separation clear? | A flat gradient presented as equivalent to a refractive or volumetric reference; arbitrary glow; mismatched shadow directions; an oversized orb reused without purpose; attractive staging that hides evidence. More depth is not automatically better for a restrained reference. |
| **Pacing and continuity** | Do type, object, camera and material tracks have distinct, intentional timing? Does camera travel support the action and settle for the result? When an object carries a transition, is its identity and path perceptually continuous? | Every element starts and stops together; workspace motion settles too early; unrelated fades replace a meaningful object transition; a jump or flash at a cut; redundant adjacent visuals without narrative progression. Watch at normal speed, then inspect boundaries and action samples by frame. |
| **Sound and listening** | After listening to the actual export, do music, silence and effects support the sequence? Are effects attached to real actions? Is meaningful source speech complete, intelligible and clear of competing audio? | An effect precedes its action, repeated clicks imply nonexistent actions, music obscures speech, audible clipping, abrupt seams or an unintentional ending. Record which file and portion were heard. Measurements cannot substitute for listening; silent visual fixtures remain **not reviewed** for sound. |
| **Side-by-side preference** | With comparable framing and playback, which film better communicates the product and sustains attention, and why? Does the output stand on its own without the reference or an explanation? | “Better” asserted from technical checks, contact sheets alone, or a different narrative range. Record **output / reference / no clear preference**, the exact compared range, and specific moments supporting the choice. Report disagreement or missing review honestly. |

## Decision and repair

Stop for rework on an unsupported claim, false UI behavior, unreadable essential information, missing action/result, broken transition, or lost meaningful speech. For a creative weakness, name the affected shot and a concrete change: for example, “continue camera travel through selection, then hold the complete result,” rather than “make it more premium.” Preserve successful source bindings and budget limits. Never mark a missing review as a pass or waive technical/factual failures because the design is appealing.

For release, retain the reviewed export and observations, resolve blocking rework, and state remaining limits. A preference judgment is a human observation, not an automatic quality guarantee.

## Current baseline and known limits — 2 October 2026

The [reference studies](reference-replication-results.md) established visible gaps in volumetric/refraction detail, material texture, object-carried transitions and independent motion timing. ClickUp's recreated workspace settled earlier than its reference; Reception's material and card transformations were approximations. The studies were manually authored and silent, so they do not prove automatic generation quality or reviewed audio.

The directed Obsidian fixture at `.local/vs204-directed-fixture/project/motion-0/picture.mp4` is a **manually authored, unpaid production-contract renderer fixture**, not a fresh end-to-end generation. Its 23.1-second silent export passed layout/state/camera and export-integrity checks. Inspection covered 32 sampled exported frames, including cut boundaries, typing, selection and readable result holds, with full-size action views. No broken essential copy or active-target crop was observed in that coverage.

It improves separate type/camera/material timing and keeps source UI styling intact. Its CSS material remains softer and less volumetric than the references, the repeated orb is less distinctive than bespoke choreography, the dense note excerpt competes with the mechanism, and the enlarged captured wordmark is slightly soft. Only the focus path was exported; connection/consolidation staging and adjacent source-identity continuity have unit coverage but still need actual visual review. Sound and whole-film side-by-side preference remain **not reviewed**. Automatic acceptance runs require their own export review; these fixture observations must not be transferred to them. See [the batch results](ENGINE-BATCH-RESULTS.md) for the separate automatic draft review and exact failures.
