# Reference-led hook development

This is the creative procedure for the next launch-film revision. It uses
observed advertising techniques as hypotheses, not as evidence of ad performance.
Reference breakdowns and their source links live in `docs/meta-ad-research` and
the `/ad-references` portfolio.

## Research record

Select actual software video creatives with an identifiable advertiser. Keep
the Meta Library ID and observed status when available; label archive-only
evidence separately. Search-result mentions are not advertiser verification.
Preserve source video privately for analysis and expose only limited storyboard
excerpts alongside criticism and attribution. A source being active or old does
not demonstrate return on ad spend, conversion or audience retention.

For every selected reference, document the opening, the product reveal, each
subsequent scene and the ending. Record the interval, visible action, transition,
and the scene's apparent communication job. Separate visible evidence from our
interpretation. State sample coverage and approximate boundaries honestly.

## Translate the mechanism

1. Name one audience and one recognizable problem or desired outcome.
2. Write the first-frame message before adding movement. It must be legible in
   silence and connect to the exact workflow the film will demonstrate.
3. Select a reference mechanism: problem interruption, visible transformation,
   outcome preview, or a question the product actually answers.
4. Choreograph the hook into the first product action. Reuse the same task,
   field or result so the opening does not feel like an unrelated title slide.
5. Add changes in scale and pace where they reveal information. Let important
   results settle before the next move.
6. Keep brand colors, source-supported controls and the demonstrated product
   claim. Borrow the structure, not another advertiser's wording or identity.

## Review and experiment

Inspect the first frame, the first half-second, two seconds, the hook-to-UI
handoff, each action/result boundary and the ending. Review a dense silent
sequence and full-size frames for competing text, discontinuity and clipping.
Technical export similarity and deterministic seeking are separate from creative
judgment. They do not establish that an audience finds an ad compelling.

Keep the prior product-motion cuts accessible and the audio unchanged in this
iteration. That permits a picture-focused comparison, although the edited
visuals are not a controlled advertising experiment. No campaign is launched by
this workflow. A later audience test should hold targeting, placement, offer and
landing page constant; compare hook retention together with downstream intent
or conversion, rather than rewarding attention without comprehension.

The current execution is manually art-directed through the existing HTML/SVG
engine. The portfolio and this procedure are reusable creative inputs; this
batch alone does not prove that automatic URL generation has learned them.
