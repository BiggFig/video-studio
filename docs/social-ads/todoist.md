# Todoist: laptop closed, brain still open

Original 18-second concept for freelancers and knowledge workers carrying tomorrow's work after the laptop closes. The campaign hypothesis is that a recognizable unfinished client task, followed by a specific saved result, gives viewers a clearer reason to try Todoist than a generic task-management message. This is a creative hypothesis; no campaign has run and no conversion improvement is claimed.
