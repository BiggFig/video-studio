# Video Studio Roadmap

Updated: September 30, 2026  
Current product scope: [PRD.md](PRD.md)

This document preserves planned capabilities outside the first MVP. Phases express a suggested order, not committed dates. Prioritize expansion using beta output quality, actual job costs, and repeat demand.

## Phase 1: Current MVP

Software launch and feature-demo videos from a product URL alone, or a PRD with assets. Optional reference, one MP4, music and SFX, automatic processing, and a free private beta.

General footage editing is explicitly outside this phase.

## Phase 2: Reference-based footage editing

Restore the original editing workflow as an additional mode:

**One uploaded raw video + reference upload or supported Instagram/TikTok link → edited video.**

- Preserve the user's speech and meaning.
- Analyze reference pacing, framing, typography, captions, transitions, motion, and sound style.
- Apply those techniques to the user's footage without reusing reference media.
- Match reference format automatically, with a format override.
- Add appropriate captions, music, and SFX.
- Return a preview and downloadable MP4 through the same job system.
- Maintain the previously agreed maximum of five minutes for finished output unless deliberately changed later.
- Keep the initial editing experience automatic, with no required creative prompting.

Reuse the unified skill, private storage, durable workers, job contract, rendering, and quality checks. The workflow remains planned even though it is removed from the launch MVP.

## Phase 3: Multiple clips and production controls

- Multiple uploaded raw clips per editing job.
- Better source selection and speech/caption timing across clips.
- User revisions and targeted regeneration.
- Editable on-screen copy and scene order.
- Optional concept/storyboard approval for users who want more control.
- Reusable brand assets and motion styles.

## Phase 4: Campaign packages

Produce related assets from shared product facts, brand assets, and an approved source project:

| Deliverable | Creative treatment |
|---|---|
| X/LinkedIn launch film | Product reveal, narrative, and meaningful proof |
| Homepage or feature demo | Clear workflow and useful explanation |
| Short Meta/Instagram ad | A distinct hook, one promise, product proof, and CTA |
| YouTube video version | Story and layout suited to its intended placement |

Generate genuinely different hooks and stories where needed. Crops and shorter exports alone do not constitute new ad concepts.

Add project history and recurring feature-release workflows. Test paid demand before selecting project pricing or subscriptions.

## Phase 5: Voice and asset libraries

- New spoken-script generation and AI voiceover.
- User-controlled voice/audio choices.
- User-owned B-roll libraries.
- Automatic tagging, search, and relevant B-roll selection.
- Licensed stock integrations where useful.

Keep real product interfaces grounded in supplied or accurately captured screens.

## Phase 6: Discovery and broader content planning

- Reference discovery for Instagram/TikTok and other supported platforms.
- Adapt the conceptual Outliers workflow to each platform's available data.
- Content planning and recommendations for recurring releases.
- Additional product categories and video formats based on validated demand.
- Publishing/scheduling integrations if users need them.

Discovery is a separate capability from producing a video. It requires its own data access and validation.

## Commercial rollout

The initial release remains a free private beta. Add paid access, project bundles, recurring plans, or service-assisted premium pilots only after measuring production costs, output quality, and willingness to pay.

No viral reach, advertising return, or customer-retention improvement is guaranteed by this roadmap.
