# Quality and delivery

Review against request and plan. Renderer success alone is insufficient.

Run `video_tool.py verify final.mp4 --plan plan.json`: full-file decode, video, fps, canvas, duration, required audio presence. Probe actual streams. Inspect mix loudness/true peaks. Reject corrupt/truncated media, wrong aspect/timing, missing speech/fonts/assets.

Inspect opening, every scene hold/seam/subtitle change, main reveal/payoff, final frame. Watch with audio. Check actual product/supportable copy/prices/names; complete thought/take order/crop; text font/wrap/reading time/contrast; actual caption words/numbers/remapped timing; purposeful motion/seek correctness; intelligible sound/no clipping; reference's important style traits and declared deviations.

Probe success does not prove semantics, suitable crops, or accurate words. Unavailable checks are not_checked. Unattended jobs need a configured visual/audio review adapter; unavailable essential review yields needs_review with actual draft. Independent reviewer gets request/profile/plan/render/criteria and returns timestamped findings, without changing the plan. Without reviewer tooling do a deliberate second inspection and record limitations.

Repair highest-severity issues in current project and plan. Up to two targeted passes by default within budget. Recheck changed visuals/audio and decode resulting file. Critical unresolved issues never pass. Record observed costs/time, not invented invoices.

Deliver final.mp4, separate poster.jpg from intentional frame, captions.srt when applicable, editable project, plan, qc, result. Poster is not silently inserted into raw footage. Authorized automatic jobs deliver after passing checks. Publishing/email/social posting remains separate.
