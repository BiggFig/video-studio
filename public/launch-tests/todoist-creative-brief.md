# Todoist — Laptop closed. Brain still open.

An original 18-second creative study for freelancers and knowledge workers who carry tomorrow's tasks after the workday ends. The goal is to connect a recognizable moment to a specific product action and a visible result. This is a proposed creative direction, not an official Todoist campaign or a measured advertising result.

## What the viewer sees

| Time | Story |
| --- | --- |
| 0–2.17 seconds | **Laptop closed. Brain still open.** A laptop closes; the thought **Send proposal tomorrow at 9am p1** interrupts. Fast movement stops so the sentence can be read. |
| 2.17–3.33 seconds | **Capture it in one sentence.** The same thought becomes the input in Quick Add. |
| 3.33–4.30 seconds | **tomorrow at 9am** moves into its date control, which becomes **Tomorrow 09:00**. |
| 4.80–5.47 seconds | **p1** becomes the **P1** priority control. |
| 6.13–7.17 seconds | The pointer approaches the real Add control and visibly presses it. |
| 7.20 seconds | The complete saved **Send proposal** task appears in **Inbox**, with **Tomorrow 09:00**, **P1**, and an unfilled priority circle. **Captured.** |
| 8.20 seconds | **Scheduled.** The saved date and time remain visible. |
| 9.53 seconds | **Prioritized.** The saved priority remains visible. |
| 11.00–14.07 seconds | **Off your mind.** A calm, complete result contrasts with the intrusive opening. |
| 14.07–18.00 seconds | **Get tomorrow out of your head.** Todoist. **Start for free.** **todoist.com** |

## Why this direction

The hook names a familiar situation before introducing the feature. One task stays consistent throughout, so the viewer can follow cause and effect. Rapid intrusion, a readable pause, purposeful word movement, a decisive click and a quiet result create contrast without new brand colors or distracting decorative objects. The message is designed to remain understandable with sound off.

Two separate compositions are provided: **1080×1920 for Reels/Stories** and **1080×1350 for feed**. They use large text and distinct placement layouts, rather than cropping the preceding landscape film. The soundtrack is unchanged so the visual revision can be judged independently.

## Product grounding

- [Todoist Quick Add](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/features/use-task-quick-add-in-todoist-va4Lhpzz) documents natural-language dates, times and priority shortcuts; available on the Beginner plan.
- [Todoist priorities](https://www.todoist.com/help/todoist/features/set-a-priority-in-todoist-Wy82Jp) documents `p1` and the red highest-priority indication.
- [Todoist](https://www.todoist.com/) supplies the **Start for free** offer and the warm neutral/red visual direction.

Sources checked October 5, 2026. The proposal is illustrative. The video shows capture and scheduling; it does not claim to send the proposal, finish the task, or provide a reminder. Word movements and interface transitions are explanatory animation rather than a screen recording.
