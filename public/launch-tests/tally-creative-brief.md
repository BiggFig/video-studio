# Tally — your next client brief, in one form

Creative study, 5 October 2026. Proposed creative for review; not an official Tally campaign or a measured advertising result.

## Audience and idea

For freelancers and small studios collecting prospective clients’ details through scattered messages. One project-enquiry form gives each person a place to provide their name, email and project request.

Hook: **“Still taking client briefs in DMs?”**

The video moves from incomplete messages to a document-style form, a visible Publish action, a completed example response, submission confirmation and a clear next step. The messages illustrate the problem; the film does not depict a DM integration or automatic message import.

## Scene breakdown

| Time | Story | What viewers see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2.5s | Still taking client briefs in DMs? | Three editorial message fragments introduce the back-and-forth. |
| 2.5–6.6s | Build it like a doc. | The Project enquiry form takes shape: Your name, Your email, What do you need? |
| 6.6–8s | Publish. Share the link. | An enlarged Publish control and a visible press. The edit then moves to respondent view. |
| 8–11.3s | Ask once. Get the details. | An illustrative client enters Ada, ada@example.com and A new landing page, then presses Submit. |
| 11.3–12.4s | Sent. | The documented default thank-you screen confirms completion. |
| 12.4–15.1s | The brief. In one place. | An EXAMPLE BRIEF card recaps those exact answers. This is an editorial summary, not a Tally dashboard or a real client response. |
| 15.1–18s | Build your client brief. | Give every enquiry a place to land. Create a free form. tally.so. |

## Delivery

- 9:16 vertical: 1080×1920, 18 seconds, 30 fps.
- 4:5 feed: 1080×1350, 18 seconds, 30 fps.
- Each format is separately composed, with large text and clear room for placement controls.
- White, ink, pale gray and functional blue remain consistent with the researched brand interface.
- The story is understandable with sound off. Existing music accompanies the film without carrying the message.

## Suggested copy

Organic caption:

> A client brief shouldn’t be scattered across messages. Give your next enquiry a place to land: name, email, project details. Build a free form with Tally.

Paid primary text:

> Still collecting client details one message at a time? Build a project enquiry form with Tally. Ask for a name, email and project details in one place. Create a free form.

Paid headline: **Your next client brief, in one form.**

Suggested button: **Sign Up**, using a brand-approved form-creation destination.

## What this should test

The hypothesis is that a specific, recognizable client-intake problem earns more qualified attention than the earlier generic hook. Compare the two messages with audience, offer and destination held constant. Read Reels and feed separately. Assess attention and click-through alongside completed form creation and product activation; views alone are not evidence that the ad acquires customers.

No customer counts, conversion gains, time savings, messaging integrations or automatic qualification are claimed. No ad account or campaign has been changed.
