# Direction lab — a fair test

Status: approved storyboard and six verified exports, 12 October 2026. No viewer comprehension results, traffic results or paid campaign results are claimed.

## 1. Review the plan before rendering

Review the Tally storyboard at `/direction-lab`. Approve the audience, factual action/result, recurring subjects, proposed framings and all three placement compositions. A storyboard is not proof that the finished animation works. Record approval and revisions in the project conversation before final rendering.

## 2. Technical and creative review

- Verify source-grounded actions and unchanged example values across states.
- Inspect every transition at dense intervals as well as all completed views.
- Check close-up target bounds, readable text, visible actions, complete result holds and the final CTA in 16:9, 4:5 and 9:16.
- Verify deterministic seeking, export decode, final media metadata and HTML/export fidelity.
- Watch at phone size with sound off. Then conduct a separate listening review. Do not label audio approved from waveform checks alone.
- Record failures and fixes in the reusable director, rather than silently patching one product film.

## 3. Five unfamiliar viewers

Use `/direction-lab/viewer`. Send the viewer route, not the storyboard route. Show one version per participant without explaining the story. Use at least five distinct people unfamiliar with this project; synthetic agents and the project team do not satisfy this condition.

Have each viewer watch once, muted, preferably at the intended screen size. Record any replay, sound-on viewing or prior familiarity. Collect their own words for:

1. Who is this for?
2. What did the person or product do?
3. What changed as a result?
4. What should an interested viewer do next?

Responses are saved only in each browser. Download and return the JSON files to the reviewer. The page does not transmit responses, verify distinct participants, or score the answers automatically.

Review rubric for the Tally pilot, kept away from participants: a complete answer identifies freelancers/small businesses collecting client details; building/publishing a form and submitting name, email and project information; a completed enquiry in one place; and creating a client-brief form at Tally. Equivalent plain language is acceptable. An answer implying automatic DM import, a sent project deliverable, or a fabricated native dashboard is a comprehension failure requiring creative review.

Proposed gate: at least four of five distinct unfamiliar viewers correctly identify all four elements. Report the individual evidence, not only the rate. Five people provide a usability signal, not a statistically established advertising lift.

## 4. Separate a historical comparison from a controlled experiment

The earlier social Tally exports are 18 seconds. The new director pilot lasts 24 seconds to make actions and results readable. The earlier landscape hook cut also has different copy. These comparisons judge the complete creative change; they do not isolate camera direction.

The matched 24-second control and treatment use the same words, source media, UI actions and states, duration, soundtrack, aspect ratio, offer and CTA. The changed variable is the complete director treatment: camera framing, composition, layer timing and materials. This pair evaluates that package. It cannot identify which individual mechanism caused a difference. Keep filenames, hashes and this comparison scope in the experiment record.

A later test can isolate one mechanism by changing only that camera, transition or timing choice while holding the other director settings fixed.

## 5. Organic and paid measurement

For an organic trial, record posting time, channel, audience, placement, caption, landing page and any other promotion. Evaluate early retention, completion and useful engagement alongside qualified visits and activation. Sequential organic posts are observational; changing distribution can explain a difference.

For a paid trial, agree the brand/account, intended audience, success event, budget, test period and stop rules before launch. Keep audience, placement, destination, attribution, offer and bid strategy constant across creative arms; use the platform's experiment controls where available. No account changes, campaigns or spend are authorized by opening this file.

Primary downstream outcome: qualified sign-up followed by the product's meaningful activation event (for the Tally concept, actual creation and publication of a relevant form, if instrumented and approved by the brand). Attention and click-through are diagnostic measures; cheap clicks without activation do not establish a winner.

Report sample sizes, spend, exposure, attribution window and uncertainty. Do not promise a conversion increase before collecting results. Preserve adverse findings and improve the engine from them.
